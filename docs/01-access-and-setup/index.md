# Access and Setup

Choose the setup that matches how you are taking the tutorial.

## Attending the live tutorial

<span class="tutorial-route-badge tutorial-route--live">Live tutorial</span>

You should have received a credential card containing:

- Your username.
- Your password.
- Your assigned tutorial server.

Continue to [**Connect to the tutorial server**](live.md).

The live environment is already prepared. You do **not** need to install Conda, TaskVine, Floability, or any other tutorial software.

## Following the tutorial on your own

<span class="tutorial-route-badge tutorial-route--self-managed">Self-managed</span>

If you are running the exercises on your own x86-64 Linux computer or server, continue to [**Create your own environment**](self-managed.md).

You will create a local Conda environment containing the tools used in the tutorial.

---

> **Live participants:** Do not install another copy of Conda or modify software under `/opt/tutorial`. Your exercises and Floability environments will be created inside your own account.
